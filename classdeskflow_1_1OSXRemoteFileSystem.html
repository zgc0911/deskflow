Detailed Description

The files that other computers have copied, as a tree of read-only directories.

The root holds one directory for each list of copied files, named after the identifier of the list, and in it the files and directories that were copied. What is inside a copied directory is asked from the other computer when it is first looked into, and the contents of a file when they are read. Nothing is transferred for files that are never pasted.

A file is downloaded in order, starting at the position of the first read. Reads may come several at a time and a little out of order, they are answered from what has arrived and wait for what has not. A read from an entirely different position starts another download.

Time passes between copying and pasting, so sizes and dates are asked for again when they are looked at after a while. A file that was changed after it was copied is pasted as it is when it is pasted. Right after a list was added its sizes and dates are taken as they are: they were current when the clipboard was sent.

Called from the threads of the NFS server, any number at a time.

bool deskflow::OSXRemoteFileSystem::shutdown ( int waitMs = 3000)

Fail every call from now on, wake the ones that wait and stop all downloads.

Waits at most waitMs for the threads that download and list to end, and returns whether they did. A listing ends when the other computer answers or the connection is gone.
