Detailed Description

Access to the files that another computer has copied.

Obtained from FileTransfer::remoteFiles(). The methods block and may be called from any thread except the one that runs the event queue. The object may outlive the FileTransfer it came from, requests then fail with Disconnected.

Ask what became of the files and directories that were copied.

Blocks until the answer is complete. entries are the top level entries of the list offerId, in the order of the list and as they are now: a copied file may have changed since, or it may be gone, which is marked.
